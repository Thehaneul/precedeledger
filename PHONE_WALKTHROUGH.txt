IF A CALL FAILS UNEXPECTEDLY
Do not repeatedly resend. Record its transaction ID and exact error. Check whether
the plan was saved using get_plan_id(creator, plan_key) and the relevant summary.
A FINALIZED transaction can still have execution ERROR. A successful execution
alone does not establish consensus acceptance or finality.
If interpretation differs or remains undetermined, preserve the evidence and
investigate the rule/prompt/code. Do not call the expected result a passed test.
